Privacy Policy

Skeptic Wise · Effective date: 25 September 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how the Skeptic Wise channel ("we", "us") handles information accessed through the YouTube API Services. It applies to the internal tooling we operate to manage our own YouTube channel and to publish our own content. These tools are used solely by the channel owner and are not distributed to third parties.

2. Information we access through YouTube API Services

When authorised by the channel owner, our tools may access:

  • Channel and video statistics (views, likes, subscriber counts);
  • Video metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails);
  • Comments and replies on our own videos;
  • The ability to upload our own videos and to post replies to comments on our own videos.

3. How we use this information

  • To analyse the performance of our own content and decide what to publish;
  • To prepare and upload our own videos;
  • To read and, where appropriate, respond to comments on our own videos.

We do not sell, rent, or share this information with advertisers or any third party. We do not use it for advertising profiling or for any purpose other than managing our own channel.

4. Storage and retention

Authorisation tokens and any cached API data are stored locally on the channel operator's own computer. They are not stored on publicly accessible servers and are not shared with third parties. Cached API data is refreshed or deleted in accordance with the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

5. Google, YouTube and third-party terms

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This application uses YouTube API Services. By using this application you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

You may revoke this application's access to your Google Account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access invalidates the stored token immediately.

To request deletion of any data we have stored, contact us at the address below. We will delete the stored authorisation tokens and any cached data within 30 days.
